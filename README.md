# 🇳🇬 Naija Life Game\n\nBrowser-based Nigerian life simulator prototype.\n\n## Features\n- ₦10,000,000 starting cash\n- Work and business income\n- Market and transport expenses\n- Energy, mood, day and location systems\n- Local save/reset\n- Animated 3D-style city scene\n- Responsive phone/PC layout\n\n## Roadmap\nVehicles, property, missions, shops, NPCs, character customization, larger 3D world and more gameplay systems.\n
